    FAQ

    Questions, answered plainly.

    The things people ask before they start tracking — no fine print, no marketing.

    Is SpendWise free to use?
    Yes. Creating an account and tracking your money costs nothing. Sign up with an email and password, or use Google sign-in for a faster start — the whole workspace is available from the first transaction.
    Do I have to connect my bank?
    No. SpendWise is manual by design: you add the entries you care about, choosing an amount, a type, a category and a date for each one. Nothing connects to your bank, and no banking credentials ever leave your hands.
    Who can see my data?
    Only you. Every account gets its own private workspace, so your transactions, budgets and summaries are visible to nobody else. The figures shown on this page are illustrative demo numbers, not anyone's real records.
    How do category budgets and alerts work?
    Set a monthly limit for a category and SpendWise tracks spending against it, filling in the progress as the month runs. When a category gets close to its cap it is flagged for review, so you can adjust before the month ends instead of after.
    What is the Financial Health Score?
    A single 0–100 reading of your month, built from your savings ratio, budget discipline and expense control. It summarises the numbers you entered — it is not a credit score and it does not look at anything outside your own activity.
    Is the investment-readiness indicator financial advice?
    No. It is educational only: it shows how a healthy savings ratio and a positive balance combine into preparation, and it never recommends a product, predicts a return or guarantees an outcome. Decisions stay entirely with you.
